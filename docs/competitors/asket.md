# ASKET

**Reviewed November 11, 2026 · broader apparel brand**

## Product facts

The white **T-Shirt** is listed at **$65 USD** for one shirt. ASKET specifies 100% organic long-staple cotton, 180gsm jersey, Portuguese production, a straight fit, and three lengths. Its catalog also includes jeans, shirts, and outerwear. [Source](#source)

## Positioning & website

The product page emphasizes construction and measurements. **Audience hypothesis:** shoppers who want detailed information before paying more for an everyday basic.

**Branding interpretation:** Sage traits appear in its educational sizing and material explanations. Reviews provide social proof. The structured product information suggests a functional, modern approach; visual style needs a separate review.

## Archetype analysis

**Primary: Sage. Secondary: Innocent. Confidence: moderate.**

ASKET’s [About page](https://www.asket.com/en-us/about) describes transparency, garment care, and a permanent collection. We read the emphasis on understanding what goes into a garment as Sage. Its promise of less clutter supports an Innocent reading centered on simplicity.

**Alternative:** repair and take-back services suggest Caregiver traits, but the overall story is more about informed, lasting ownership than nurturing alone. These are our readings, not ASKET’s declared archetypes.

**Competitive implication:** a new brand cannot match this position just by using a minimal page. It needs useful product evidence and a promise it can sustain.

Brand-story source accessed November 11, 2026. [Full archetype comparison](/research/archetypes) · [Competitive analysis](/research/competitive-analysis).

## Key takeaway

Make quality and fit specific: measurements and material details are more useful than an unsupported “premium” claim.

**Still to test:** opacity, wash durability, and whether shoppers find the extra length choice helpful.
