# Customer research

## Purpose

Learn why people buy plain white T-shirts, what disappoints them, and how they choose. No interviews or surveys have been conducted yet.

## Interview prompts

1. Tell me about the last plain white T-shirt you bought.
2. What did you buy it for, and what other options did you consider?
3. What mattered most when deciding?
4. What, if anything, disappointed you after wearing or washing it?
5. How did you choose your size?
6. What would make you buy the same shirt again?
7. What did you pay, and did that feel worth it? Why?

Ask about real behavior before asking hypothetical willingness to pay. Avoid leading questions like “Would you prefer our better-quality shirt?”

## Notes format

Use an anonymous participant ID, date, shopping context, consent to record notes, key observations, and limitations. Do not publish contact details or recordings here. Small convenience samples are exploratory, not representative market estimates.
