# Research brief

## What we are building toward

Eventually, we want to create a clothing brand centered on a plain white T-shirt. Right now, we are collecting evidence to understand the competitive landscape before making brand decisions.

## Working scope

- **Product:** plain white short-sleeve T-shirt; begin with crew-neck products for comparability.
- **Market:** United States online shopping, USD, as a working assumption.
- **Customer:** not yet selected. Start with comparable adult products; document marketed gender and fit instead of mixing categories silently.
- **Business models:** prioritize truly single-product and T-shirt-focused brands; use broader retailers as reference points.
- **Out of scope for now:** storefront, checkout, sourcing contracts, finished brand identity, and sales forecasts.

## Questions we need to answer

1. Who competes for the same purchase, and how narrow is each brand's assortment?
2. What do shoppers pay per shirt, including pack requirements and delivery conditions?
3. Which promises recur: fit, opacity, softness, durability, origin, sustainability, or value?
4. What evidence supports those promises?
5. How do product pages make choosing a size and buying easier or harder?
6. What unresolved needs could our future product address?

## Outputs

A verified competitor directory, comparable profiles, a source-backed matrix, customer research notes, and a short opportunity brief. A promising idea remains a hypothesis until we test it.
