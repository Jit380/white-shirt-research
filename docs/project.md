# Our project

**Goal:** create a brand and website selling plain white T-shirts. We will make the decisions below after researching the competitive landscape.

| Later document | What we will decide |
| --- | --- |
| Customer persona | Who we serve, what they need, and what affects their purchase |
| Brand direction | Our positioning, brand archetype, and evidence-based use of Robert Cialdini’s persuasion principles |
| Design direction | Whether a modern, postmodern, or blended approach fits the audience and brand |
| Website & coding plan | Pages, buying flow, features, technology, accessibility, and implementation tasks |

These are placeholders, not decisions. Add the documents here when the research supports them.

## How we work

1. Collect competitor evidence in the [research section](/competitors/).
2. Discuss feedback and track work in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Jit380/white-shirt-research/issues).
3. Turn findings into brand decisions, then a website plan.

To suggest a change, use the link below. Include what should change and why; screenshots and source links help. GitHub sign-in is required, and issues are public. Suggestions are reviewed before being implemented.
